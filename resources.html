Classroom Teaching Resources

Simple ideas that teachers can use during classroom lessons.

📘

Teaching Guide

Introduce mathematical concepts using familiar objects, examples and classroom discussions.

  • Explain concepts step by step.
  • Use objects available in the classroom.
  • Encourage children to answer together.
🧮

Number Cards

Use number cards to help children recognise, compare and arrange numbers.

  • Show numbers from 1 to 100.
  • Ask children to arrange numbers.
  • Practise odd and even numbers.
🍎

Learning with Objects

Connect mathematics with everyday objects that children can see and count.

  • Use fruits for addition.
  • Use pencils for subtraction.
  • Share objects to explain division.

LEARN TOGETHER

Quick Classroom Activity Ideas

Activities that encourage children to participate as a group.

01

Number Hunt

Ask children to identify numbers around the classroom, such as page numbers, dates and labels.

02

Count and Add

Place two groups of objects on a table. Ask children to count them and find the total.

03

Share Equally

Give a group of objects and ask children to divide them equally among a few groups.
